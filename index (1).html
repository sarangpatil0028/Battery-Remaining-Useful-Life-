Battery Fleet Explorer

Interactive look at the 200-battery Remaining Useful Life (RUL) dataset: what the fleet is made of, and how it is split for model training.

Data note: the supplied files hold battery metadata and train/validation/test lists only. They contain no cycle counts, capacity-fade readings or RUL values, so this dashboard describes the fleet and the split, not battery life.
